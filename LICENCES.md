# Third-party licensing notes

No third-party audio engine is bundled in prototype 0.1. The project currently uses Android/Jetpack Compose dependencies declared in `app/build.gradle.kts`.

Rubber Band Library is not included. Its official licensing page states GPL v2-or-later for open-source distribution and commercial licensing for proprietary applications. Review current terms before integration or distribution: https://breakfastquay.com/rubberband/license.html

The app must retain required notices for all AndroidX/Jetpack and other dependencies when distributing a built APK.
